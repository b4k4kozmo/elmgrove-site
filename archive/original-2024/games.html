Shoujou Showdown

Shoujo Showdown is one of our oldest games and the first game we entered in a game jam! We made this game using Stencyl in only 30 days, which at the time was a great feat for us. SheepCreep

Party Crashers TD

A bunch of woodland partygoers are trying to crash your cabin for a wild rager! Fend them off by creating mazes. Party Crashers TD is a tower defense with good vibes and kicked back beats, made in collaboration with Odd Boss games. Chill out and have some relaxing fun by downloading on itch.io!


MetaPizza Delivery
